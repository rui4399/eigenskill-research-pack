## How To Read These Files

- Mentor reports and paper drafts here may contain speculative architecture
  language.
- The swarm, acoustic communication, physical assembly, and nonlinear
  eigen-routing sections are future ideas, not completed experiments.
- The edge-runtime drafts do not yet include ARM/NPU board latency or energy
  measurements.
- The current strongest reproducible artifact is the quantization-policy v1
  deterministic bypass track, plus the older v2 routing PoC with its overlap
  limitation stated explicitly.

Do not cite files in this directory as empirical evidence unless the claim is
also supported by committed data, scripts, or output summaries in the repository
root, `data_eval/`, `train_python/`, `inference_cpp/`, or `outputs/`.
